I am a design researcher exploring the intersection of generative AI, human-AI interaction, and creative practice. With a background in fashion design and garment technology, I am currently a Ph.D. Candidate in Design at UNIST, where I study how AI can support creative professionals in ideation, visual exploration, and decision-making. My work combines user research, design experimentation, prototyping, and generative AI to develop more human-centered creative tools and workflows. Beyond academia, I have experience in commercial fashion design, project management, and industry collaboration, with a particular interest in translating emerging technologies into meaningful user experiences.
